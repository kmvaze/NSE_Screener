📖 Institutional Quant Playbook & Strategy Masterclass

Welcome to the NSE Alpha Pro Terminal. This terminal is engineered to solve the single greatest challenge in stock investing: consistently identifying market-outperforming stocks across every market regime without emotional guesswork.

1. 🏠 Landing Dashboard: Market Regimes, Stickiness & The Top 12 Grid

Markets do not move in a straight line; they continuously transition across four distinct environments—from raging bull markets to choppy corrections and steep bear markets. A trading strategy that works during a speculative boom often leads to heavy drawdowns during a market downturn.

To solve this, our Market Leadership Command Grid does not use a rigid, static formula. Instead, it continuously measures the broader health of the entire National Stock Exchange:

  • Market Breadth: Tracks what percentage of the entire market is actively trading above its medium-term trendline (50-day average).
  • Market Relative Strength (MRS): Measures whether the benchmark index is accelerating or stretching dangerously far from its baseline.

Based on these conditions, the engine automatically identifies the current Market Regime and dynamically shifts capital:

  • 🟢 Expansion Tape (Healthy Market): The market is trending up with broad participation. The system aggressively prioritizes fast-moving leaders and high-momentum compounders.
  • ⚠️ Overheated Tape (Stretched Market): The market has risen too far, too fast. The system reduces risk by rotating out of extended stocks and positioning into calm dip-buying opportunities and defensive anchors.
  • 🛡️ Bearish Defense (Market Correction / Weak Tape): Most stocks are falling below their trendlines. The engine locks into capital preservation, exclusively selecting low-risk, defensive anchors that big institutions are quietly protecting, alongside deeply oversold setups.
  • 🔄 Early Recovery Thrust (Market Turning Up): The engine spots the earliest signs of market bottoming and positions ahead of the crowd in emerging industry groups.
Why the "Top 12" Are Bound to Outperform Most Stocks:

The 12 featured stocks on your dashboard are not random screen results. They undergo strict diversification constraints: no single sector can occupy more than 2 slots. This eliminates portfolio concentration risk while ensuring that every chosen company has cleared elite filters for price tightness, institutional delivery absorption, and structural support.

Signal Stickiness & Turnover Stability: Instead of whipsawing your portfolio with new tickers every single day, our engine uses persistence smoothing. When a stock enters the Top 12, it has demonstrated multi-week institutional accumulation, giving it strong structural retention so you can trade calmly without unnecessary portfolio turnover.

2. 🎯 The 4 Complementary Quant Strategies (Layman's Guide)

Every stock in our database is evaluated against four distinct, professional trading setups. Each serves a specific purpose in your wealth-building portfolio:

🛡️ Strategy 1: High-Surety Institutional Anchor

Purpose: Capital defense and steady compounding.

In Simple Words: Identifies resilient stocks coiling calmly near their key moving averages while big institutions quietly absorb selling pressure. These stocks act as defensive anchors, exhibiting very small drawdowns even when the broader market experiences volatile swings.

🚀 Strategy 2: Momentum Frontrunner

Purpose: Maximum trend velocity and rapid gains.

In Simple Words: Zeros in on the strongest stocks in the entire market that are consistently making fresh multi-month highs. When market conditions expand, these top-tier leaders surge forward with the highest upward speed.

🔄 Strategy 3: Early Sector Rotation Leader

Purpose: First-mover advantage before the crowd arrives.

In Simple Words: Big money constantly rotates capital from tired industries into fresh, newly emerging sectors. This strategy pinpoints the exact leaders of an industry group just as institutional funds begin flowing in, positioning you ahead of sector-wide rallies.

📉 Strategy 4: High-RS Pullback Structure

Purpose: Low-risk entry on proven market champions.

In Simple Words: The best stocks never go straight up; they take breathers. This strategy waits for an elite market leader to experience a shallow, low-volume dip back to its key support line with oversold momentum, offering an asymmetrical, low-risk buying opportunity.

3. 📊 Technical Screener Tab: Decoding Scores & Finding Your Own Stocks

While the Landing Dashboard presents the curated Top 12, the Technical Screener Tab opens the entire market universe to you. Here, you can run custom scans, apply specific index universes (like Nifty 50, Midcap 150, or Microcap 250), or drill down by strategy.

Understanding Key Columns in Plain English:

  • Strategy Score (0 to 100): The overall health rating for the selected strategy. A score above 75 indicates strong technical alignment with that strategy's setup rules.
  • IASIS Flow (Institutional Accumulation & Squeeze Index): Measures whether big institutional buyers are quietly locking up shares while price volatility dries up. A high score signals that a stock is wound like a spring and ready to expand.
  • Pattern / Confluence: Confirms genuine technical chart patterns—such as coiled springs, high-tight flags, or multi-month accumulation bases—backed by heavy trading floor support.
  • RS Status (Relative Strength): Compares the stock's strength directly against the broader Nifty index. A stock printing a fresh RS High is moving up even when the broader market is declining.
  • RRR (Reward-to-Risk Ratio): Calculates potential profit relative to downside risk. A 3:1 ratio means you are aiming for ₹3 of potential profit for every ₹1 of risk taken.
  • 50/50 Staggered Split Entry: Instead of buying everything at once, our system calculates a Tranche 1 entry at market trigger and a Tranche 2 limit entry at a discount. This lowers your average purchase price and cuts typical drawdown risk in half.

4. 🌐 Sector & RS Analysis Tab: Spotting Industry Waves

Individual stocks rarely move in complete isolation; over 50% of an individual stock's move is driven by the strength of its entire sector. The Sector Tab is one of the most critical tabs in the entire terminal:

  • Relative Strength Spread Curves: Instantly visualize which industries are beating the market baseline (0.00%) over rolling 20-day, 63-day, and multi-month windows. Sectors curving sharply upward are receiving institutional cash inflows.
  • Interactive Sector Tiles: See real-time advance/decline health, 20-day percentage performance, and the percentage of sector stocks trading above their 50-day moving averages.
  • Constituent Drilldown: Clicking on any sector tile instantly unlocks all individual companies operating within that sector. You can plot stock relative strength curves against their peer group to immediately pinpoint the true leaders driving that sector's rally.

5. ⏪ Quantitative Backtest Tab: Empirical Audit & Performance Metrics

The Backtest tab is your objective proof of performance. It runs point-in-time forward evaluations across 10 distinct monthly cohorts using official exchange Bhavcopy and delivery data without lookahead bias:

  • Peak Profit Expansion (MFE): Evaluates the maximum profit potential each trade setup achieved after the buy signal before exiting. Across all strategies, average peak expansion exceeds +28%.
  • Max Adverse Drawdown (MAE): Evaluates how far a stock dipped below its entry price before moving upward, helping you verify that your stop-loss will not be hit prematurely.
  • Method 3 (50:50 Staggered Execution): By entering half a position at trigger and placing a limit order for the second half at a discount, backtest audits verify that win rates surge from 63.0% to 83.6%, with capital expectancy reaching over +₹22,700 per ₹1 Lakh allocated.
  • Month-on-Month Cohorts: Switch between monthly signal dates (from Jan 2026 to Oct 2026) to see the exact setups selected at each turn and how they performed forward in time.
  • Strategy & Market-Cap Filtering: Easily toggle the backtest view strategy-wise (Surety, Momentum, Rotation, Pullback) or by company size (Large Cap, Mid/Small Cap, Microcap) to see which segments generated the highest market outperformance.

6. ⭐ Watchlist Tab: Personal Setup Tracking

The Watchlist is your personalized command center. Click the star icon (⭐) beside any company on the Landing Grid, Technical Screener, Pattern Scanner, or Sector Drilldown to save it to your private list:

  • Multi-Timeframe Alignment: Review all your tracked stocks in one consolidated view to check if their short-term and medium-term price trends are aligned.
  • Cloud & Local Synchronization: Your starred stocks stay saved across sessions, so you can track setups as they develop toward their optimal entry trigger.
  • Maintenance Tools: Use the built-in Clean Missing Tickers feature to instantly keep your active watchlist pruned and focused strictly on live exchange candidates.
