📖 Institutional Quant Playbook & Strategy Masterclass

Official technical guidelines and dual-archetype execution rules for the NSE Pro Engine.

Archetype 1: Early Base Ignition ("Catch the Next Run-Up")

Target stocks completing quiet accumulation within high-tight bases before institutional markup begins. Look for:

  • Volatility Compression: Bollinger Bands contracting completely inside Keltner Channels (TTM Squeeze).
  • Moving Average Pinch: 10 EMA, 21 EMA, and 50 SMA spread contracting to $\le 2.8\%$.
  • Supply Exhaustion: Volume Dry-Up (5-day volume $< 65\%$ of 50-day average) on narrow bar spreads.
  • RS Line Divergence: Relative Strength line breaking out to a 63-day high while price trades under horizontal base resistance.

Archetype 2: Momentum Frontrunners ("Ride Proven Winners")

Target established Stage-2 market leaders with strong multi-horizon relative strength outperformance. Look for:

  • Trend Alignment: Price $> 10 \text{ EMA} > 21 \text{ EMA} > 50 \text{ SMA} > 200 \text{ SMA}$ with upward-sloping 200-day moving average.
  • Volume Flow: 50-day Up/Down volume ratio exceeding $1.30$.
  • Pullback Entry: Enter on low-volume retests of the rising 10 EMA or 21 EMA rather than chasing breakouts extended $> 8\%$ above base averages.
