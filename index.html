📖 Institutional Quant Playbook & Strategy Masterclass

Welcome to the official strategy guide for the NSE Pro Quant Engine. This terminal integrates real institutional data pipelines, volume profile analysis, sector performance matrices, and multi-factor fundamental scoring to help you consistently identify market outperformers.

🚀 Part 1: Technical Outperformance Playbook (Short to Medium Term)

The Technical Screener is designed to capture market leaders and momentum setups in any market regime (Bullish or Bearish). Follow these step-by-step rules:

  1. Check Market Regime: Verify the market breadth status at the top header. In a 🟢 Bullish Regime, aggressive breakout and buy-zone strategies thrive. In a bearish regime, restrict trades to high-RS pullbacks.
  2. Scan Sector Analysis Matrix: Open the Sector Analysis tab. Focus exclusively on leading sectors with high advance percentages and robust % of stocks trading above their moving average.
  3. Filter High Alpha & RS New Highs: On the Technical Screener, sort by Alpha Score descending and look for stocks with 🔥 RS Line New High status. This guarantees you are buying stocks outperforming Nifty.
  4. Execute on Tactical Setups:
    • 🎯 VCP Coiled Spring: Price volatility has dried up after an advance; ideal for entering right before an explosive breakout.
    • 🟢 Low-Risk Buy Zone: Stocks consolidating safely near their 20-day moving average with positive momentum.
    • ⚡ Stealth Accumulation: Heavy institutional delivery volume spikes while price remains tightly coiled.

💼 Part 2: Fundamental Compounding Playbook (Medium to Long Term)

The Fundamental Screener is engineered for long-term wealth creation by identifying cash-generating compounders with superior operating metrics:

  1. Filter for Elite Scores: Use the ⭐ Elite ≥ 80 tactical button or type `80` in the Min Elite Score filter to isolate top-tier companies.
  2. Target High ROE & Growth: Set minimum thresholds for ROE > 20% and YoY Growth > 20% to ensure high capital efficiency and rapid earnings compounding.
  3. Check Shareholding Filters: Use the Promoter, FII, and DII holding filter inputs to verify institutional backing and management skin in the game.
  4. Validate Institutional Accumulation & FCF: Confirm positive Free Cash Flow (FCF) Yield and growing institutional sponsorship to ensure the company is self-funding its growth.

⚡ Part 3: High-Conviction Confluence Strategy (The Ultimate Edge)

The highest probability trades occur when technical strength and fundamental excellence overlap:

  • Step 1: Screen for stocks on the Fundamental Tab with an Elite Grade (A+ Elite) and positive earnings revisions.
  • Step 2: Cross-reference those symbols on the Technical Tab to ensure they show a VCP Coiled Spring or Low-Risk Buy Zone setup with an Alpha Score > 70.
  • Step 3: Check that the stock belongs to an outperforming sector on the Sector Analysis tab.
  • Step 4: Enter the trade using the calculated ATR Stop Loss and target a Risk-Reward Ratio (RRR) of at least 3:1.
